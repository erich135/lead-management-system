Description:

CARD 11: Conversion Tracker - "Complete Workflow Only" Toggle
================================================================================

Title: Add "Complete Workflow Only" Toggle to Conversion Tracker

Description:
Added a toggle to filter conversion metrics to only show jobs that have completed the entire workflow. This ensures segment times add up to the total.

Problem Solved:
Previously, segment averages didn't add up to total because each segment was calculated from different job sets (some jobs have dateQuoted but not poDate, etc.)

Solution:
- "Complete Workflow Only" checkbox
- When enabled, only includes jobs with ALL dates present
- Segment totals now mathematically add up to the total time
- Shows count of jobs with complete workflow data

Files Modified:
